# Content strategy: Demo Labs

## Cadence
Four posts a week, Monday to Thursday, at 10:00. Friday is the weakest day for B2B.
Posting more often than this cannibalises the reach of each individual post.

## Format mix
- Two image cards a week: one point, clearly visualised.
- One text card: a claim that stands on its own.
- One carousel every other week, when the material carries several points.

## Principle
Quality over volume. One post that gets remembered beats four that get scrolled
past. If we have nothing to say in a given week, we say nothing.
