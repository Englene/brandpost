# Design style: Demo Labs

Flat vector shapes on a calm, light background. Scandinavian minimalism: plenty of
air, few elements, no shadows or gradients.

- The primary colour fills the large, load-bearing shapes. The secondary tone is
  support only.
- Three to four elements in the motif, maximum. More than that: merge or drop.
- No photorealism, no desk scenes, no coffee cups.
- Numbers and labels short enough to read on a phone, in motion.
