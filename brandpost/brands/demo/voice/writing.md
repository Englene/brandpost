# Writing style: Demo Labs

Calm, concrete, no sales voice. We explain something worth knowing and let the
reader draw the conclusion. Write like you would explain it to a colleague who is
short on time.

- Open with something concrete: a number, a situation, a sentence someone said.
- Short paragraphs. One point per paragraph.
- Every number needs a source or a clear boundary. No vague approximations.
- End with a question only when you actually want an answer.

## Anti-patterns (that AI smell)

These four moves are what make text smell like a machine. They are banned:

1. **Do not build the point on the antithesis** "not X, but Y". "You do not write
   the application. You sharpen it." That reads like an ad, not a person. This
   applies to closing lines too.
2. **At most one dramatic one-word paragraph** per post, preferably none.
3. **Do not open with what "most people think"** and then knock it down. Get to
   the point.
4. **Let the number carry the weight.** Do not explain how surprising it is.

## Personality

Dry wit is welcome, but it has to be anchored in something concrete. Test: could
a customer quote the sentence in a meeting without cringing? Then it is fine.
